remaining non-closed items are concentrated in these areas:

- boundary implementation and open-niche pressure provenance;
- historical permeability endpoint geometry and repeated-support likelihood policy;
- final NMR free-water/bound-water or anomaly residual policy;
- ERT transform, support mask, and covariance;
- Taupe/TDR unit, calibration, baseline, and grouped uncertainty;
- RH active-curve source, sensor screening, Kelvin constants, extension, and boundary
  uncertainty policy;

  reference/sign conventions, quality flags, and covariance;
- fault/fracture/crack 3D-to-2D projection, aperture/effect, and quantitative use;
- CTE provenance and prestress magnitude/orientation/excavation-initialization confirmation.
